# THE NEWSPAPER ROOM

> a tiny editorial studio for curious creators ♡

**The Newspaper Room** is a browser-based newspaper and editorial publishing tool where you can create, edit, design, and export your own digital newspaper pages.

Designed to feel like a little independent publishing desk, the project combines **editorial typography, newspaper layouts, creative controls, and interactive page editing** into one playful frontend experience.

---

## FEATURES

### Multi-page newspaper editor

Create different sections of your publication:

* **01 — Front Page**
* **02 — Tech & Culture**
* **03 — Features**

Each page has its own editorial layout and content.

### Live editing

Edit your newspaper directly from the editor panel.

Changes appear directly in the newspaper preview.

### Newspaper templates

Choose from different editorial starting points:

* The Daily Byte
* Midnight Herald
* The Archive
* Culture Paper
* The Weekly
* Deadline

### Aa Typography controls

Experiment with different visual styles:

* Classic
* Modern
* Editorial
* Mono

### Paper themes

Change the appearance of your newspaper:

* White
* Warm
* Grey
* Black

## ✦ PAGE SYSTEM

The newspaper is split into three editorial sections.

### 01 / FRONT PAGE

The main entrance to the publication.

Includes:

* Lead story
* Breaking news
* Featured story
* Sidebar
* Pull quote
* Editor's note

### 02 / TECH & CULTURE

A magazine-style page focused on technology and culture.

Includes:

* Main technology story
* Culture article
* Trend report
* Quick reads
* Editorial quote

### 03 / FEATURES

A long-form editorial layout.

Includes:

* Feature headline
* Introduction
* Long-read content
* Pull quote
* Interview section
